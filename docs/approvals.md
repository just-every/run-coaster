Each employee has one approving manager. When they submit a claim, it waits for that manager under **Approvals**.

## Review a claim

Open **Approvals**. **Waiting for you** lists the claims to decide, with their number, total and person. Open one to see every expense, its receipt (choose the thumbnail to open it full size), its category split and any policy exception with the employee's explanation.

## Decide

Under **Decision**:

- **Approve** sends the claim on to finance. If it has policy exceptions, first tick that you reviewed them and the employee's explanations.
- **Return for correction** sends it back to the employee. Write the reason, for example "Attach the Union Station Grill receipt".
- **Reject** closes it. Write the reason.

The reason is required to return or reject. Your decision applies to the exact revision you are looking at: if the employee changed the claim while you were reading it, Coaster asks you to reload.

Nobody approves their own claim. A manager's own claims go to their own approving manager.

## Away from work

Before you are away, open **Approvals** and, under **Delegation**, choose another manager and the dates. Claims submitted while the delegation runs go to them; they decide with the same rules, and the claim and its history say they approved on your behalf. Claims already waiting stay with you. Choose **End** to stop it early.

The owner can move a waiting claim to another manager with **Reassign** on the claim; the history records who moved it, from whom, to whom and why. When the owner deactivates a manager, the claims waiting for them move to that manager's own approving manager, each with a history line.

## After the decision

The claim's history records who decided, when, and why. An approved claim appears for finance under **Batches**, ready to be paid. Next: [Reimbursement batches](/docs/batches).
