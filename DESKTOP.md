# QuickBooks Desktop

Open Settings, then QuickBooks Desktop. Enter the full company file path on the QuickBooks computer and explicitly authorize hourly accounting reads and posting. Save the one-time password privately and download the connector file. Install it in QuickBooks Web Connector, open that company, enter the password and enable its hourly schedule. The hosted installation needs HTTPS. Reconnecting retains the original company identity and posting history.

The connector pulls accounts, classes, customers, vendors and employees. Under Desktop mappings, choose the Coaster expense account, employee vendor, company card, accounts payable or recorded-payment bank account and select its active QuickBooks entry. Existing cost centers and closed-statement journal accounts are offered by name. The owner, admin and finance may connect; the owner, finance and bookkeeper may queue sync, map entries and retry a refused request. Delegates and API credentials cannot operate this connection.

Each scheduled Web Connector authentication queues catalog reads, reviewed reimbursement batches, approved company-card expenses, recorded batch payments and the non-purchase entries of closed card statements. Bills sum to the reimbursement batch; card charges preserve their original amount and remain outside reimbursement. Native statement purchases are represented by those card charges, so their journal lines are not posted again. Bill payments record payments finance already recorded in Coaster; they do not release funds. Existing CSV, XLSX and IIF exports remain available.

A QuickBooks refusal retains its original message. Correct the mapping and choose Retry failed request; successful financial requests keep their source identity and are never resent. A lost response is marked uncertain and requires reconciliation in Desktop before any repost; automatic retry never guesses that a write failed. A response must match the outstanding request identity and operation. Disconnect stops new connector sessions and invalidates existing tickets.

Connector passwords are stored only as hashes. Passwords and session tickets never enter an archive. Catalogs, mappings, request outcomes and numbered audit history survive export and restore; a restored connection must be explicitly reconnected to the same company. An outstanding write restored without its response becomes uncertain. Financial identities follow ERASURE-001's opaque retained subject.

The accepted proof uses a synthetic Web Connector at the production SOAP door. `tests/coaster-qbo.test.mjs` exercises catalog, batch bills, card charges, balanced journals, recorded bill payments, replay prevention, original error messages, isolated retry and native role refusals. `tests/browser-desktop.mjs` drives configuration, catalog, mapping, failed and successful posting states at 1440 and 390 and performs an exact public CLI export and restore from a fresh installation at the final migration into an empty installation. The journey requires connector setup and sync history to be present, then compares every history field and every portable record exactly after re-export; identity, catalog, mappings and request outcomes survive while credentials and tickets remain excluded. This bounded Desktop proof does not complete ACCT-001 or ACCT-006.
