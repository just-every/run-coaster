# Product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Coaster is expense management that replaces Expensify, SAP Concur Expense or Emburse for a US company of 15–150 employees, with the card, policy-agent and conversational depth buyers now look to Ramp, Navan and Brex for. Receipts are snapped, forwarded, texted or pulled from inboxes and read automatically; corporate-card and bank feeds are matched to receipts; policy is enforced as people spend and submit; approvals chase the people who owe them; reimbursements go out by ACH through a payment provider and are tracked to settlement; and every expense, receipt, reimbursement and clearing entry syncs both ways with QuickBooks Online, Xero or Pantry. Money is USD by default, distance is miles at the IRS standard mileage rate, per diem follows GSA rates, and schedules respect US time zones and business days.

The product is the whole expense lifecycle the incumbents sell: capture, cards and feeds, claims, coding, policy, approval, follow-up, reimbursement, accounting, reporting, administration, mobile and migration from each incumbent. Neighboring products stay separate and are integrated, never rebuilt: Pantry is the ledger, Purse owns supplier purchasing and bills, Willow holds the HR record, Gauge bills clients for project time and expenses, Nook is team chat, Auth is workforce sign-in and Crank runs cross-product automations. Payroll is run by the buyer's payroll provider or [Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope). No gap in implementation reduces this destination: provider applications, card programs, ACH onboarding and native apps are engineering work recorded as clauses below.

This is a target contract, not a release verdict. Every named clause states the behavior a buyer gets and its acceptance evidence: the journey or test through a public door (screen, HTTP API, agent API, CLI or provider webhook) that proves it. The [Status](#status) section marks each clause done, partial or missing at current code; a cited local test does not establish hosted operation, a live provider connection or an accessibility certification. Existing clause IDs COA-001 to COA-013 are preserved and their flow tests in `tests/coaster.test.mjs` remain their proof; `tests/browser.mjs` drives the same flows through the screens at 1440 and 390 pixels wide. This documentation round changes no homepage, interface or runtime behavior.

## Existing guarantees: the claim-to-reimbursement spine

- **COA-001 — Business, categories and people set by the owner.** The owner names the business and sets its currency (fixed once claims exist), timezone, mileage rate, optional per-diem rate and receipt threshold; adds categories with account code, tax code and optional per-expense limit; and adds people as employee, manager and/or finance with each employee's approving manager. Nobody else can change these. LOCALE-001 sets the US defaults and ADMIN-001 widens the roles. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-001` flow test: the owner configures the business, categories and people through the HTTP API; a manager, finance member and employee are each refused the same writes and nothing changes.

- **COA-002 — Receipt, mileage and per-diem expenses with receipt provenance.** An employee starts a claim and adds purchases with a receipt photo or PDF, mileage (distance × the business rate, rounded half up once) and per-diem days (days × the business rate). Each receipt keeps its exact bytes, SHA-256, size, type (from its own signature), uploader and time. Invalid dates, amounts and files are refused and change nothing. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-002` flow test: upload a JPEG and a PDF, add mileage and per diem, read back exact bytes and hashes, and see malformed dates, amounts and disguised files refused with no write.

- **COA-003 — Exact splits across categories and cost centers.** An employee splits one expense across categories and cost centers; the parts must add up exactly to the expense, and each part becomes its own accounting line. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-003` flow test: split one expense three ways, see a non-adding split refused, and read three coded accounting lines in the batch export.

- **COA-004 — Policy exceptions explained, acknowledged and carried to the books.** Coaster flags policy exceptions (no receipt above the threshold, an expense's total in a category over that category's limit however it is split, the same receipt twice, the same date/merchant/amount as another expense on the claim, another claim or an imported report). Submission needs the employee's explanation for each; the manager must acknowledge them to approve; they stay on the decision, the batch and the accounting CSV. POLICY-001 extends the rules. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-004` flow test: submit an over-limit, receipt-less and duplicate claim, see submission refused without explanations, approval refused without acknowledgment, and the exceptions in the batch and CSV.

- **COA-005 — An exact duplicate is never reimbursed twice.** One database key per expense (its receipt bytes' SHA-256, or without a receipt its employee, date, type, merchant and amount) admits it to one batch only, and a claim holding the same exact expense twice cannot be submitted. FEED-004 extends the same key to card transactions. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-005` flow test: approve two claims carrying the same receipt and see the second refused from any batch; a claim holding the same expense twice cannot be submitted.

- **COA-006 — The assigned approver decides the exact submitted revision.** Submission freezes the claim as a numbered revision and routes it to the employee's approving manager. Only that approver approves, returns (with a reason) or rejects (with a reason) that exact revision; nobody decides their own claim; a stale view is refused. APPROVE-001 extends routing to multi-step workflows, APPROVE-005 lets the approver hold single expenses and approve the rest, and APPROVE-007 adds retraction and unapproval before payment, all without weakening these rules. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-006` flow test: another manager, the employee and a stale revision are refused; the assigned manager's decision is recorded against the exact revision.

- **COA-007 — Corrections never erase evidence.** A returned claim is corrected and resubmitted as the next revision. Earlier revisions, their receipts and decisions stay readable and downloadable; receipts, revisions, decisions and history lines cannot be changed or deleted. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-007` flow test: return, correct with a replacement receipt and resubmit; both revisions, both receipts and every decision remain readable and no route edits or deletes them.

- **COA-008 — Role-scoped access in every handler.** Access follows roles in every API handler, not only in screens: employees see their own claims; approvers see claims routed to them once submitted; finance and the owner see submitted claims; drafts stay private to their employee. Writes need the current native session from the same origin; deactivated people (in Coaster or in their sign-in account) lose access at once, agent keys included; forged fields in requests decide nothing. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-008` flow test: each role reads and writes across every claim, batch and receipt route; cross-origin, deactivated and forged requests are refused with no write.

- **COA-009 — Reviewed reimbursement batches with reconciled exports.** Finance gathers approved claims into a draft batch, reviews it by confirming the reconciled total (someone else must review a batch holding their own claim), then downloads an accounting CSV (one line per category split with account/tax codes, receipt SHA-256, exceptions and approver) and a reimbursements CSV (one total per person). Both add up to the batch total. Once the batch is released under PAY-004's controls, finance can record a payment made outside Coaster with its reference and date; PAY-001 is the provider-paid route and ACCT-001 to ACCT-003 the live ledger route, and this file route stays available alongside them. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-009` flow test: batch, review, download both CSVs, check both sum to the batch total, record the payment and see each employee's claim marked reimbursed.

- **COA-010 — Personal agents prepare; people decide.** A person makes an agent key. Their agent prepares draft claims from receipts (employees) or batch proposals (finance) through `/api/coaster/agent/*`; it cannot submit, approve, batch, pay or read anything else. Only that person accepts or declines; acceptance is recorded with who and when, and the claim or batch says it was prepared by the agent. The push-further agents (AGENT-001 to AGENT-009) build on this authority model. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-010` flow test: an agent key proposes a claim and a batch, is refused submission, approval and foreign reads, and only its person accepts; the provenance appears on the result.

- **COA-011 — Expensify report history move-in.** The owner moves in Expensify history from a report-template CSV (IMPORTING.md) on Base import-kit: preview writes nothing and lists errors; commit applies only the exact previewed bytes and mapping; the original file is retained; undo reverses the job, and the same file can be imported again after an undo. Imported reports stay read-only history and flag later re-claims as possible duplicates. MOVE-001 extends this to the whole Expensify account. **Acceptance evidence:** `COA-011` flow test: preview with errors, commit, see a re-claim flagged, undo and re-import the same file.

- **COA-012 — Whole-installation export and restore.** The owner exports the whole installation with `rer export` and restores it into an empty installation with `rer import`: claims, revisions, decisions, receipt bytes, batches, history and imported history come back identical, and a corrupt archive or one with inconsistent claims is refused without writing. Provider connections and secrets added by later clauses are excluded and must be reconnected (SEC-001). **Acceptance evidence:** `tests/browser.mjs` archive section and `tests/browser-archive.mjs`: export, refuse tampered and inconsistent archives, import into a second empty installation and compare.

- **COA-013 — Fresh-clone install and runtime.** Coaster installs and runs from a fresh clone with `rer install`, `setup`, `dev`, `build`, `preview`, `verify`, `reset`, `export`, `import`, `deploy --dry-run`, `describe` and `check`, on TanStack Start, Hono, D1 and Cloudflare Workers. **Acceptance evidence:** `tests/browser.mjs`, `rer check` and `rer verify` on the exact commit.

## Target clauses

### US defaults

- **LOCALE-001 — US-first defaults.** A new installation defaults to USD, a US time zone chosen from the US zones offered at setup, miles, US date and number formats and a receipt threshold of $75 (the IRS documentary-evidence line). A category whose name says lodging, hotel or motel always needs a receipt, and the owner cannot turn that off; every other category has an owner-set “Always needs a receipt” flag. The mileage rate is an effective-dated table seeded with the IRS standard business mileage rate for each year, editable by the owner, and each mileage expense takes the rate in force on its trip date. Receipts carry sales tax as its own field mapped to a tax code. Other currencies and kilometers remain available per entity (ADMIN-004). Later entities may use any IANA time zone through the API (ADMIN-004). **Acceptance evidence:** Set up a fresh installation and see USD, miles, the US time-zone choices and the $75 receipt threshold. For categories named lodging, hotel and motel, verify a receipt is always required and the owner cannot disable that rule; for another category, set and clear “Always needs a receipt” and verify the flag controls its below-threshold receipt requirement. Use the API to set a non-US IANA time zone for a later entity (ADMIN-004) and verify it is retained. Enter mileage dated either side of a January rate change and see each priced at its year's rate; capture a receipt's sales tax and see it on its own accounting line.

### Capture

- **CAPTURE-001 — Photo, file and bulk receipt upload.** On phone or desktop an employee photographs a receipt with the camera or uploads JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or multi-page PDF files up to 10 MB each, one at a time or dozens at once by drag and drop. Each upload lands in the employee's receipt inbox with its processing state visible (received, reading, read, needs attention) and keeps COA-002 provenance; large files live in R2 with their hash in D1. Exact intake semantics: 10 MB means an inclusive 10,000,000 bytes per file; each file is committed independently, so a refused file never removes successful uploads. Receipt format comes from its signature, not its extension or browser type. Until CAPTURE-003 ships, a received original immediately enters needs attention with the reason that reading is unavailable; no reading or read success is claimed. The camera source records use of the camera control, not verification that a physical camera took the image. The original bytes, hash, uploader, source, time and private numbered state history survive COA-012 export and restore. **Acceptance evidence:** At 390 px photograph a receipt; at 1440 px drop 30 mixed files including an 8 MB three-page hotel folio PDF and a HEIC; see each appear in the inbox with state, exact bytes and hash, and a 12 MB file refused with a reason.

- **CAPTURE-002 — Email-in receipts.** Each installation has a receipts address. Mail from a member's known address (or an alias they confirm) becomes receipts in that member's inbox: attachments, inline images and HTML receipt bodies rendered to a stored PDF. Mail from unknown senders is held for the owner, never attached to anyone. Forwarded receipts keep the original message headers as provenance. AGENT-002 obligation: merchant-email receipt fields that disagree with the reading or attached charge use the same one-question, confirmed-only correction and source-evidence service. **Acceptance evidence:** Forward an airline HTML receipt, a PDF invoice and a photo from a member's address and see three inbox receipts with the source message retained; send one from an unknown address and see it held; confirm an alias and see the next one land.

- **CAPTURE-003 — Automatic receipt reading with a correction path.** Every receipt is read for merchant, date, total, subtotal, sales tax, tip, currency, payment card last four and line items, each with a confidence and the image region it came from. Read fields prefill the expense; low-confidence or conflicting fields are marked for the employee, who corrects them in place, and the correction and the original reading are both retained. A field is shown as confident only when the reader's confidence clears the installation's threshold and it agrees with every other source (card charge, email receipt, line-item sum); anything else is marked for review and never silently prefilled as settled. Reading runs through the Base AI module with an optional Amazon Textract AnalyzeExpense adapter, and failures show an actionable state rather than a silent blank. Exact native door semantic (sx-coaster-c37, 22 Oct; external-processing consent and reading-retention default awaiting ct): the direct Base AI transport and the owner/admin setting both default off. An employee or live Submit-only/Full delegate explicitly consents to one original’s reading attempt; no upload automatically sends it. One configured vision model processes the original, with no retry or provider fallback. The private numbered reading retains field confidence, source regions, normalized values, review reasons and each human correction; originals and reading history remain under the existing opaque-identity financial-record retention rule. Only safe fields prefill a draft expense or explicitly selected card-match details. The employee alone can enable the off-by-default standing matching choice with the sharing notice; it uses safe fields and never resurrects an individually withdrawn, discarded, merged or cash-reserved original. Reading does not submit, approve or pay. Textract and automatic processing of unconsented originals are not implemented. Reading quality is measured, not asserted: a committed, labeled corpus of at least 40 synthetic US receipts (`tests/fixtures/receipts/` with one label file per receipt giving merchant, date, total, subtotal, sales tax, tip, currency and card last four) covers restaurant receipts with handwritten and printed tips, hotel folios, faded thermal receipts, airline and ride-share email receipts, multi-page PDF invoices, crumpled and skewed photos and a foreign-currency receipt, and every reader adapter is scored against it. Obligation carried from CAPTURE-004 (scope-review 67): reading may prefill an original's match details, but automatic card matching uses only details the person confirmed or fields this clause marks confident; reading never opts an original in. A person may choose a standing setting that opts in all of their own read originals; it is off by default and nobody else can set it. **Acceptance evidence:** Run the reading suite over the labeled corpus through the public upload door: (1) every field the reader marks confident equals its label exactly (zero confident-wrong fields); (2) every field that differs from its label is marked for review, never prefilled as confident; (3) each prefilled expense's amount equals the labeled total before the employee touches it whenever the total was marked confident, including the tipped restaurant receipt (total includes the tip), the hotel folio (folio balance, not the first night) and the faded thermal receipt; (4) the per-field read-without-correction rate for merchant, date and total is reported and recorded with the commit, and merchant, date and total are read correctly without review on at least 90% of the corpus. Then correct the faded total and see both values in history; force the reader to fail and see the needs-attention state with a retry.

- **CAPTURE-004 — Receipt inbox and automatic attachment.** Unattached receipts wait in the employee's inbox and are attached automatically to the matching card transaction (FEED-004) or turned into a draft out-of-pocket expense when no card charge matches within a configurable window. The employee can attach, detach, merge duplicates or discard with a reason; discarded receipts stay in history. Exact rules today: an employee with their own active employee membership, or their live ADMIN-005 Submit-only or Full delegate, can discard a private inbox original with a trimmed nonblank reason of at most 1000 characters and its current lifecycle sequence (zero before any disposition). The same-origin native session, live grant, employee ownership and exact sequence are checked atomically; agent keys and role-only owner, finance, approver or auditor access grant no discard authority. Discard is terminal and allowed in any reader state or under a legal hold: a private append-only lifecycle line records sequence, kind, reason, time, signed-in actor, on-behalf employee and app channel, separate from unchanged reader history. Replay, stale and concurrent second discards are refused with 409; a foreign employee sees 404. The original remains downloadable in Discarded receipts with its exact hash and provenance; no un-discard or expiry starts, and re-upload creates a new original. Future attachment, matching and draft automation must skip discarded receipts: this clause's automatic attachment and cash-draft window, and FEED-004's receipt matching, carry that obligation before they ship. Private inbox lifecycle lines and reasons never join global history, audit packs, claim data or notifications; only the whole installation archive carries them. Unshared originals and filenames stay in the employee and delegate inbox scope. Under the 21 Oct attachment notice below, an attached receipt's ordinary provenance, including its filename, is shared with approvers and finance once submitted; its private lifecycle and reasons remain separate. Future attachment, matching and draft automation must skip merged originals as well. A duplicate merge uses the same authority and atomic checks: the employee, or their live Submit-only or Full delegate, merges one of that employee's own active inbox originals into another of their own originals (never itself, a foreign original, or one that is already discarded or merged) by naming the survivor and the duplicate's current lifecycle sequence, with a trimmed nonblank reason of at most 1000 characters unless both originals have the same SHA-256 (then the stored reason is empty unless one is given). A merge is terminal like discard, allowed in any reader state or under a legal hold, and writes one private append-only lifecycle line (kind merged, survivor, reason, time, signed-in actor, on-behalf employee, app channel) on the merged original only: the survivor, both originals' bytes and hashes and the reader history are not written, the merged original stays downloadable in Merged receipts, and it cannot be un-merged or later discarded; a later discard of the survivor leaves the merge line alone. Replay, stale sequence and the loser of concurrent merges (including opposite directions) are refused with 409, a foreign original or survivor with 404. Merge lines follow the same privacy, retention and erasure rules as discards: no marker column or reason reaches approvers, finance, global history, audit packs, claim data or notifications, names and addresses in a merge reason are replaced under ERASURE-001 (an empty reason is never rewritten and is not counted), and archives refuse a merge whose survivor is unknown, itself, another employee's, closed before the merge, merged back, or lacking a reason for different files. Automatic card attachment (ct scope-review 67, 21 Oct): until CAPTURE-003 reads receipts, automation considers only originals whose employee, or that employee's live Submit-only or Full delegate, has entered the merchant, purchase date, total and currency (card last four optional) on the private original and chosen to match it to their card charge, after seeing that approvers and finance can see the receipt, its file name and the entered total, currency and date once the matched expense is submitted, including by scheduled submission. Entering details changes no reader state and claims no reading. Details and the choice can be changed or withdrawn while the original is unattached, each as a private sequence-guarded lifecycle line; agent keys and role-only owner, finance, approver, card-admin or auditor access have no such authority. FEED-004's rule attaches such an original only to the same employee's posted, receipt-less card expense on an editable draft or returned claim when each is the other's only candidate, through the same atomic transition as a manual attach (claim version, lifecycle sequence, one R2 original, no copied bytes); otherwise the employee and their live delegates get a private question instead of a guess. Automation skips discarded, merged, attached, cash-draft-reserved and withdrawn originals, undone or rejected pairs and inactive members; it never submits, notifies or changes a card expense's statement facts, and it writes a private attached line by "automation: card match" with the rule version on behalf of the employee. While opted in, an original is outside the cash-draft window; an original with a cash-draft reservation cannot be opted in and is attached by hand. Undo is a detach by the employee or a live delegate while the expense is editable; that pair is never proposed again and matching stops for the original until it is chosen again. Before submission no original, file name, hash, entered detail, question or private line reaches anyone outside the employee and delegate scope, global history, audit packs, reports or notifications. An employee or live Submit-only or Full delegate can attach an active unattached inbox original to a purchase without a receipt on their own draft or returned claim, and detach it while the expense remains editable. Each operation checks the claim version and original lifecycle sequence atomically, appends a private attached or detached line naming claim, expense, actor and on-behalf employee, and advances the claim version. Attaching shows that approvers and finance can see the receipt once submitted. Claim aliases retain provenance and reference the single R2 original without copying its bytes; detaching preserves earlier submitted receipt evidence. Attached originals cannot be merged or discarded until detached. Generic expense editing cannot bypass these transitions. Financial retention may remove the expense while its private lifecycle references and original remain; when no live expense carries it, the original can be attached again without altering those historical lines. The cash-window preparation slice opens an empty native draft claim and a private dated needs-details record; the employee enters its amount and category, then attaches its original through these same controls. When they ship (ct, 21 Oct): attach, detach and merge are only by the employee or their live Submit-only or Full delegate on that employee's own originals and draft or returned expenses, with the person and delegate actor on the private lifecycle line and the shown notice at attach that approvers and finance see the receipt once the expense is submitted (COA-008); the cash-draft window defaults off, finance sets 1-90 days and must confirm the shown count of existing older unattached originals before any draft is made, and the hourly automation is idempotent and replay-safe, bounded, once per original, dates the draft at upload with no amount and needs-details, never submits it, skips discarded and merged originals, stores the original once with its hash preserved and records the audit actor automation: cash-draft window. Turning the setting off stops future preparation and keeps existing drafts and reservations. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a receipt before its card charge exists, enter its details and choose matching, then post the charge through FEED-002's statement-file import and see it attach automatically once, by "automation: card match"; see two same-amount candidates raise a private question, an undone match never return, and a discarded or merged original stay unmatched (the live-feed forms of this journey belong to FEED-001, FEED-002's program feeds and CARD-001); upload a cash receipt and see a draft expense after the window; discard one and find it in history; merge a duplicate into another receipt and find it in Merged receipts. Automatic attachment (scope-review 67): through public doors, as the employee and both live delegate levels, enter details and opt in (notice text checked at 1440/390), import a synthetic statement fixture whose charge then attaches once with a private automation line and a global charge-only FEED-004 line; refuse opt-in by agent keys, role-only owner/finance/approver/auditor, foreign employees, revoked delegates, stale sequences and cash-reserved originals; prove skips for discarded, merged, attached, withdrawn, undone and inactive-member cases and for another employee's same-amount charge; two candidates produce one private question and no attach, and answering attaches by the person; replayed and concurrent hourly deliveries attach once; finance, approvers, auditors, history search, audit pack, reports and notifications show no original, file name, hash, entered detail or question before submission, and the ordinary provenance plus match basis after; opted-in originals stay outside the cash window; archive round trip identical and malformed match archives refused; ERASURE-001 replaces identities on the new private lines. Obligation carried from ERASURE-001: discard and merge inputs use text(reason,Reason,1000), measured in UTF-16 code units before trimming. - **CAPTURE-005 — Inbox retrieval from Gmail and Outlook.** With an employee's consent, Coaster connects to their Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailbox with read-only scopes limited to receipt searches, finds receipts for their unmatched card transactions and attaches them, naming the message each came from. ### Cards and feeds

- **FEED-001 — Card and bank connections through Plaid.** Finance connects company card and bank accounts, and employees connect personal cards for reimbursable spend, through Plaid Link. Transactions arrive through Plaid's incremental sync cursor, assigned to cardholders by account and last four, including pending-to-posted transitions and reversals without duplicates. Obligation carried from MOVE-004: a feed charge matching imported Ramp, Brex or Navan history (same cardholder, date, merchant and amount) is not created again. Obligation carried from ERASURE-001: erasing a member removes their personal-card connections in the same erasure, keeping the expenses already created. Obligation carried from AUDIT-001: each connection, disconnection and sync appends its numbered history line (actor, agent, channel, time) that no route edits or deletes and that survives export and restore. Obligation carried from CAPTURE-004 (scope-review 67): a waiting opted-in inbox receipt never attaches to a pending transaction; it attaches under FEED-004's rule when the sync posts the charge, and the pending-to-posted transition keeps one expense and at most one attachment. Obligation carried from ADMIN-001: connecting and disconnecting company feeds follows the role permission matrix's connect row (owner, admin, finance), and every other role is refused with 403 and no write; an employee connects only their own personal card. Exact rules today: a comment is on the claim or on one of its expenses and never changes the claim's status or version; a question names one person who can open the claim (its employee, its approver, finance, an auditor or the owner), never the asker, and stays open until that person or their delegate replies in Coaster. The named person is emailed through the installation's mail provider, and the answer to the request says whether the provider accepted the message or mail is not set up; nothing claims a send that did not happen. Each claim row counts its open questions, the approver's queue included. The author may edit a comment until anyone replies, the earlier wording stays in the history line, and comments are never deleted; a delegate comments in the granting person's name, recorded as theirs on that person's behalf (ADMIN-005). Answers from chat or mail replies, notification preferences and chasing do not exist yet, so each clause that adds one carries this obligation before it ships: APPROVE-003 and CAPTURE-006 take answers from Slack and Teams replies, CAPTURE-002 from email replies, NOTIFY-001 delivers by each person's chosen channel, FOLLOWUP-001 chases open questions, and APPROVE-005 holds and FEED-004 card charges take comments the same way. **Acceptance evidence:** As an approver ask "Which client was this for?" on one expense of a pending claim; see the claim stay pending with the question marked open, the employee notified, the employee answer from a Slack reply, and the approver approve with the answer visible; try to edit the question after the reply and be refused.

- **APPROVE-007 — Retract a submitted claim and unapprove before payment.** An employee, or their delegate (ADMIN-005), can retract a submitted claim before any approver has decided it, for example to add a forgotten receipt. The claim returns to draft, the retraction is recorded on that revision, it leaves every pending approver's queue and their reminders stop, and resubmitting makes the next revision (COA-007). Once any step has approved, retraction is refused and the employee asks the approver to return it. The approver who approved, finance or an admin can unapprove an approved claim with a reason until it is in a reviewed batch, released for payment (PAY-004) or posted into a closed ledger period. The claim goes back to the step that approved it, or to the employee, as the person unapproving chooses; anything ACCT-001's at-approval trigger already posted to the ledger is voided or reversed there; and the history keeps the original approval and the unapproval with who, when and why. After a batch is reviewed or released, unapproval is refused and corrections follow ACCT-006. Exact rules today: with one approval step, "before any approver has decided" means the claim is still waiting for its approver; the retraction is a history line naming the revision it ends, and the claim leaves the approver's queue. "An admin" is ADMIN-001's admin role, which the owner also holds. "The approver who approved" is the person who recorded the approval or the approver it was recorded on behalf of (APPROVE-002, ADMIN-005). An approved claim can be unapproved while it is in no batch or in a draft batch not yet reviewed, and unapproving takes it out of that batch; once its batch is reviewed, unapproval is refused. Sent back to the approver, the same frozen expenses become the next revision for a fresh decision (COA-006); sent to the employee, the claim is returned for correction. PAY-004's release comes only after review, so a released claim is already refused. No ledger postings, card charges or ledger periods exist yet, so each clause that adds one carries this obligation before it ships: FOLLOWUP-001 stops a retracted claim's reminders, APPROVE-001 sends an unapproval back to the step that approved, ACCT-001, ACCT-002 and ACCT-003 void or reverse an at-approval posting on unapproval (card charges arrive with FEED-004), and ACCT-006 refuses it in a closed period. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a claim and retract it before any decision; add a receipt, resubmit, and see revision 2 with the retraction recorded on revision 1 and the approver's pending reminder stopped; another employee's attempt to retract it is refused; retract a claim after its first step has approved and be refused. ### Follow-up and automation

- **FOLLOWUP-001 — Reminders that chase people.** Coaster reminds employees about missing receipts, unsubmitted expenses, held expenses (APPROVE-005) and unanswered questions (APPROVE-006, AGENT-002), and approvers about pending decisions, on a schedule finance sets (for example after 2, 5 and 10 business days), through each person's chosen channels (NOTIFY-001), respecting their time zone, quiet hours and US federal holidays. Each reminder links straight to the one thing to do and stops the moment it is done. Obligations this clause carries from clauses that shipped before it: the held claim an APPROVE-005 hold creates is chased with its employee until they resubmit it or the hold is released; an open APPROVE-006 question is chased with the person it names until they or their delegate reply; and retracting a claim (APPROVE-007) stops its approver's pending reminders at once. Exact rules today: finance or the owner sets one to five rising whole numbers of business days (2, 5 and 10 by default) and the quiet hours (20:00 to 08:00 by default, in the business time zone). The open items are a receipt expense on a draft or returned claim with no receipt where one is needed (over the receipt threshold or in an always-receipt category), each draft or returned claim holding expenses, each held claim not yet resubmitted, each question not yet answered by the person it names or their delegate, and each submitted claim waiting for its approver (a new revision or a reassignment starts afresh). The hourly run notes each item the first time it sees it open; on a US business day (Monday to Friday except the observed federal holidays) outside quiet hours, an item whose business days since that day reach the next step is reminded once for that step (when several are due at once, only the latest), by email to the person who owes it with a link to its claim, and each reminder is a history line on the claim. An item stops being reminded the moment it is no longer open: a receipt attached, a claim submitted, a hold resubmitted or released, a question answered, a decision made or the claim retracted. Chosen channels and each person's own time zone and quiet hours do not exist yet, so NOTIFY-001 carries them before it ships, AGENT-002's questions are chased like APPROVE-006's when it ships, and FOLLOWUP-002 escalates an exhausted sequence. **Acceptance evidence:** With a clock-controlled installation, leave a receipt missing, a draft unsubmitted, a held expense unanswered and a claim unapproved; advance the clock and see reminders on the scheduled business days, none on a holiday or in quiet hours; resolve each and see its reminders stop. - **FOLLOWUP-002 — Escalation with service levels.** When a reminder sequence is exhausted, the item escalates to the person's manager, then to finance, as finance configures; approval service levels (for example three business days) show on the queue and in reports (REPORT-002). Escalations are recorded on the item. Exact rules today: finance or the owner sets the approval service level (3 business days by default) and the escalation gap (2 business days by default; empty turns escalation off). Business days count as in FOLLOWUP-001, from the first hourly run that saw the item open. A claim waiting for its approver escalates on the first business day past the service level, any other open item once its last reminder step plus the gap has passed; each goes first to the owing person's approving manager, then after another gap to everyone in finance except that person, or straight to finance once when the person has no active manager; when finance has set an ADMIN-001 escalation chain for the owing person's department, or a default chain, that chain replaces this one and each step records the chain version. Each escalation is sent once by email with a link to the claim, on business days outside quiet hours, and is a history line on the claim naming the level, the recipients, who owed it and the business days; it stops when the item is done. The approvals queue shows each waiting claim's business days since submission and how many are past the service level, and REPORT-002's cycle-time report counts the decisions made more business days after submission than the service level allows. **Acceptance evidence:** Let an approval pass its three-day service level and see escalation to the approver's manager and then finance, each recorded; approve it and see escalation stop.

- **FOLLOWUP-003 — Missing-evidence chasing on card spend.** Card charges without a receipt, memo or required field are chased automatically from the day they post, with the option to freeze an issued card (CARD-001) after a configured number of days and unfreeze it when the evidence arrives. **Acceptance evidence:** Post a charge with no receipt, advance seven days and see three reminders and the card frozen; attach the receipt and see the card unfreeze and the chase close. This slice performs no issuer operation.

- **NOTIFY-001 — Notification delivery and preferences.** Coaster sends notifications for submission, decision, return, payment, failure, reminder and escalation events by email (Base mail adapter with Postmark), Slack, Microsoft Teams, Nook and mobile push. Each person chooses channels per event type, digest or immediate; delivery attempts and provider acceptance are recorded per message, and a failed channel falls through to email. Obligation carried from ADMIN-001: connecting the Slack, Teams, Nook, push and mail delivery providers follows the role permission matrix's connect row (owner, admin, finance), and every other role is refused with 403 and no write; each person still chooses their own channels. - **AUTO-001 — Automatic reports and scheduled submission.** Expenses group into reports automatically (by period, trip or card statement) and submit on a schedule the owner sets (daily, weekly, twice monthly, monthly) when they are complete and compliant; incomplete items stay behind with their reminders. Exact rules today: an expense an employee adds without choosing a claim goes into their open automatic report for the approved spend request (trip, CLAIM-005) it is linked to, otherwise for its expense date's calendar month, and a report is opened when none is; claims built by hand are never grouped or submitted for anyone, and grouping by card statement joins with FEED-005. Then each automatic draft report submits to the employee's approving manager, recorded as submitted by "schedule", with its complete expenses: an expense is incomplete while a policy exception on it is unexplained (a missing receipt included), a required field (CLAIM-007) is blank or it repeats another on the report, and complete otherwise (an explained exception goes to the approver as COA-004 sets out). Incomplete expenses move with their receipts to a new automatic report for the same month or trip, the move and each reason are recorded, and FOLLOWUP-001 keeps reminding about them; a report with nothing complete, or whose employee has no approving manager, waits. **Acceptance evidence:** Set weekly submission; add five compliant expenses and one missing a receipt; at the scheduled time see a report of five submitted and the sixth held with a reminder. See an expense linked to an approved trip collect in that trip's report, and a claim built by hand stay unsubmitted. Grouping by card statement is in FEED-005's acceptance.

### Accounting

- **ACCT-001 — QuickBooks Online two-way sync.** Coaster connects to QuickBooks Online by OAuth, pulls accounts, classes, locations, departments, customers, vendors and employees, and posts card expenses as purchases on the card account, reimbursable expenses as bills to the employee as vendor (or journal entries, as configured), payments as bill payments from the funding account, and every receipt as an attachment. ## Honest boundaries

- Coaster is not a bank, card issuer or lender. Company cards are issued by the issuing partner and its sponsor bank (CARD-001), which hold the funds, run business verification and own credit decisions; Coaster runs the controls and the expense workflow. Existing cards from any issuer stay usable through feeds. Commercial card feed enrollment (FEED-002) is completed with each issuer on the issuer's timetable and forms; Coaster supplies the delivery endpoint, keys and checklist and tracks each step, and statement import covers any days before the first file arrives.
- Coaster does not hold reimbursement funds or operate ACH. Dwolla (or another provider) and its bank partners move the money under Nacha rules, with their own onboarding and timing; Coaster owns approval, release controls, status and reconciliation.
- Coaster never asks for or stores bank sign-in credentials. Feeds come through Plaid and card program files, institution coverage is shown, and statement import is the recovery path; no feed is promised to be universal or instant.
- Coaster does not run payroll or file taxes. Payroll belongs to [Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope), the RunEditRun payroll product, or the buyer's Gusto, ADP, Paychex or Rippling (PAY-003); 1099 filing, taxability and deductibility decisions stay with the buyer's accountant or Pantry; Coaster supplies flags and records, not tax advice.
- Coaster is not a travel agency. Booking stays in Navan, TravelPerk, SAP Concur Travel or Egencia, and Coaster takes their itineraries and receipts (INTEG-001).
- Coaster is not the ledger, the procurement system, the HR record, the PSA or the chat tool: those are Pantry, Purse, Willow, Gauge and Nook (or the buyer's QuickBooks Online, Xero, Procurify, BambooHR, Harvest, Slack or Teams), connected through SIBLING-001 and the named integrations.
- The US is the first market: USD, IRS and GSA rules, US holidays and US providers. Foreign-currency receipts and additional entities are supported (CLAIM-003, ADMIN-004); foreign VAT reclaim is done by a specialist reclaim partner from Coaster's exported receipts.
- Native app store distribution, provider applications (Plaid, Dwolla, issuing partner, QuickBooks, Xero, Slack, Teams) and card program enrollment are engineering and partnership work tracked by their clauses, not cuts in scope. No SOC 2 report, PCI scope reduction or hosted service level is claimed without its own evidence.
- No agent verdict replaces a person's decision to release money, change a policy or act on a fraud signal, and raw records stay readable without any AI summary.

## Decided defaults

- Money is integer minor units; each mileage amount rounds half up once; the rate used is copied onto the expense.
- A missing receipt above the threshold is a soft exception that needs an explanation, unless finance makes it hard (POLICY-001). Exceptions never block approval on their own: the approver decides, except that an unlinked expense over the spend request threshold needs the approver's recorded override reason (CLAIM-005).
- "Exact duplicate" means the same receipt bytes, or for an expense without a receipt the same employee, date, type, merchant and amount; the same card transaction ID for card spend. Same date, merchant and amount with different receipts is a visible possible duplicate, not a block.
- One finance person may create and review a batch unless it holds their own claim; dual release applies above the PAY-004 threshold.
- Receipts and their history are never deleted outside RETAIN-001 and ERASURE-001.
- Agent keys and provider credentials are per installation, stored hashed or encrypted, and excluded from exports; a restored installation reconnects its providers and issues new keys.
- An archive carries Coaster's data, not sign-in accounts or sessions: roles are keyed by email, so after a restore people sign in to the new installation and keep their roles.
